Artisan Technique

Step-by-Step Preparation

Crafted through a careful 6-step process ensuring quality in every single bite.

Step 01 01

Wash

Hugasan ang kamote nang mabuti.

Wash the sweet potatoes thoroughly under clean water to remove all surface soil and impurities.

Step 02 02

Peel & Boil

Balatan at pakuluan ang kamote hanggang lumambot.

Carefully peel the sweet potatoes and boil them in hot water until thoroughly softened.

Step 03 03

Mash

Durogin ang cooked kamote nang pino.

Mash the boiled sweet potatoes until smooth, fine, and lump-free.

Step 04 04

Mix

Haluan ng Doreen condensed milk para sa tamis at lasa.

Add Doreen sweetened condensed milk into the mashed sweet potatoes for sweet, creamy consistency.

Step 05 05

Wrap & Fill

I-wrap sa lumpia wrapper at lagyan ng cheese sa gitna.

Scoop sweet potato onto a lumpia wrapper, place a cheese stick in the middle, and seal tightly.

Step 06 06

Fry

Prituhin sa mainit na mantika hanggang maging golden brown.

Deep fry in hot cooking oil until the wrapper turns crisp and beautifully golden brown.
